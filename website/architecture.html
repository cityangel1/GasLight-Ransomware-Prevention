Recovery is always too late.

Ransomware wins the moment encryption starts. Traditional tools try to clean up afterward — GasLight is built to never let that moment arrive.

05 · DECEPTION ENGINE

Bait it can't resist.

Ransomware traverses directories alphabetically or hunts for high-value keywords. GasLight exploits that habit by planting irresistible decoys exactly where it's looking.

🜂 Why alphabetical seeding works

Most automated ransomware sorts and encrypts files alphabetically within a directory. Naming a decoy file 00_ or _0_ guarantees it's the very first thing the malware thread evaluates.

+98% confidence — a touch on any bait is treated as near-certain malicious intent, since no legitimate process has a reason to open it.
